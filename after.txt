Installation Complete! Thank you for installing LightBar For Keyboard.

Quick Tips:
1. To access settings, just RIGHT-CLICK anywhere on the white light bar.
2. Double-click the light bar to instantly toggle light boost, or scroll your mouse wheel to adjust height dynamically.
3. From the right-click menu, you can select height presets, revert to default (40px), change colors, or switch between "Docked" and "Floating" modes.
4. If you chose to create a desktop shortcut, you can start the app from there anytime.
